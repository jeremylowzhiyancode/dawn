# Hospital Onboarding Plan

**Purpose:** A clear way to take a hospital from “interested” to “live and using the platform,” without losing momentum after they say yes.

**How we work day to day:** use the Excel checklist for each hospital’s next steps. This document is the simple overall plan.

---

## The path

### 1. Interest
Hospital has seen the product and signed the needed papers.

**Ready to move on when:** demo done, LOI signed, EAA signed.

### 2. Preparation
We book kickoff quickly so the hospital is not left waiting.

**Ready to move on when:** kickoff invite sent and meeting scheduled.

### 3. Kickoff
We walk them through the platform, set up their admin access, and complete one practice run together.

**Ready to move on when:** first practice is complete.

### 4. Go live
Hospital finishes their profile. We publish it once agreed. Optionally, they run a first real feasibility with a CRO.

**Ready to move on when:** profile is live (and ideally first real pilot is done).

### 5. Follow-up
We check in at 2 weeks, then 1 / 2 / 3 months, and move toward a commercial agreement.

**Ready to move on when:** check-ins are done and the commercial path is clear.

---

## Simple rules

1. One person on our team owns each hospital.
2. Every hospital always has one clear next action.
3. We always know if we are waiting on **us** or on the **hospital**. Waiting on us is the risk we protect against.
